ghostWriter terminology for Spanish review

ghostWriter: ghostWriter. Product name; do not translate.
Markdown: Markdown. Technology name; do not translate.
Render: Renderizar. Use consistently for the app action.
File Actions: Acciones de archivo. Name of the editor menu.
Document language: Idioma del documento. Describes exported content, not the app interface.
Braille code: Código braille. Includes language and contraction level.
Unified English Braille: Braille inglés unificado.
Spanish braille: Braille español.
Grade 1: Grado 1. Uncontracted braille.
Grade 2: Grado 2. Contracted braille.
eBraille: eBraille. Format name; do not translate.
Braille Ready Format: Formato Braille Ready. BRF format name.
Recently Deleted: Eliminados recientemente.
VoiceOver: VoiceOver. Apple product name; do not translate.

The translator may replace a preferred term. Record that decision in the workbook so it can be applied consistently throughout the app.
