Spanish braille output review

This review is separate from the interface translation review and should be completed by someone qualified to read Spanish braille.

Review both Spanish braille choices:

1. Spanish braille, grade 1 (uncontracted).
2. Spanish braille, grade 2 (contracted).

Use representative Spanish documents containing:

- Accented vowels, including á, é, í, ó, and ú.
- Ü and ñ in upper- and lowercase words.
- Opening and closing question marks and exclamation marks.
- Numbers, decimals, dates, and punctuation next to numbers.
- Uppercase words, abbreviations, and mixed letters and numbers.
- Headings, paragraphs, emphasis, lists, task lists, links, and tables.
- Page wrapping and page breaks in BRF.

For eBraille, also confirm that the title, document language, headings, navigation, task states, links, tables, and any tactile-graphic descriptions are meaningful in Spanish.

Record the selected braille code, the source sentence, the observed braille, the expected braille, and a short explanation for every issue.
