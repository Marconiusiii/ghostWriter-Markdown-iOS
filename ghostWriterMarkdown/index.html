Write in Markdown. Never lose your place.

ghostWriter is a lightweight editor that keeps long documents approachable, turns Markdown into polished pages, and makes the tools you need easy to find.

The app has been submitted to the App Store for review.

Try ghostWriter on the web View the project

A clear path through every draft

Whether you are jotting down a short note or shaping a long article, ghostWriter keeps the writing space simple while useful tools remain close at hand.

Your document has a map

Outline gathers every heading into one useful list. Choose one and ghostWriter takes you straight to that point in the document, so a long draft never becomes a maze.

Know where you are

A customizable Status Bar can show the current line and column, line count, word count, character count, and other details you care about.

Markdown made handy

Insert Actions help with headings, links, web images, lists, quotations, code, and more. Use them with selected text or begin something new.

Files stay organized

Search, sort, pin, rename, duplicate, delete, and restore documents from a straightforward Library.

Share the finished result

Preview your Markdown as a polished page, then share the original Markdown, plain text, or a complete web page document.

Write plainly. Render beautifully.

Your Markdown stays comfortable to edit, while Render gives you a clean view of the finished document. Headings, lists, links, quotations, and other structure carry through to the result.

When it is ready to leave ghostWriter, share it in the format that fits the moment.

Make the editor yours

Choose the font and appearance you find most comfortable. Adjust the Status Bar, automatic lists, keyboard shortcuts, and sound without crowding the editor itself.

ghostWriter gives you useful choices, then gets out of the way.

Designed to work well from the beginning

Clear organization, meaningful control names, scalable text, strong contrast, keyboard support, predictable focus, and thoughtful VoiceOver behavior are part of ghostWriter’s design—not extras added after the fact.

The result is an editor that respects how different people read, navigate, and write while keeping the experience pleasantly uncomplicated.

Private by design

ghostWriter does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking. Your documents are stored with the app unless you choose to export or share them.

If you add an image from the web, ghostWriter uses the address you provide when displaying that image. The details are available in the Privacy Policy.

Why ghostWriter?

The original ghostWriter began as a mischievous web experiment that quietly changed a few words whenever a document was rendered. It eventually grew into a real Markdown editor and teaching tool.

The iOS app leaves your words exactly where you put them—for now.
